# Onboarding Guide

## Day one
Collect your laptop and access card from the IT desk between 09:00 and 11:00 IST.
Your corporate identity is created before day one; the temporary password is handed over in person.
Complete the security awareness module in the first 48 hours.

## First week
Your manager assigns a buddy for the first 30 days.
Join the team stand-up, the #helpdesk channel and your project's Confluence space.
Request access to Jira, Confluence, GitHub and the HR portal through the IT helpdesk if anything is missing.

## Accounts checklist
Corporate email, single sign-on, VPN, password manager, Jira, Confluence, GitHub and the HR portal.
Access to production systems requires a separate approval from the engineering manager.

## Probation
The standard probation period is 3 months.
A confirmation review happens in the last week of probation.

## Where to ask
HR questions: hr@company.example
IT questions: the IT helpdesk channel or helpdesk@company.example
Payroll questions: payroll@company.example
