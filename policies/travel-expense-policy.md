# Travel and Expense Policy

## Eligibility
Business travel must be approved by the reporting manager before booking.
Bookings made through the corporate travel desk are paid directly and need no reimbursement.

## Per diem
Domestic per diem is 1500 INR per day, covering meals and local transport.
International per diem follows the country rate table published on the finance portal.
Per diem is not payable on days when meals are provided by the company or the event.

## Reimbursement
Claims above 5000 INR require manager approval before the expense is incurred.
Original receipts are mandatory for every claim.
Claims must be submitted within 15 days of the expense date; late claims need finance head approval.

## Cab and local transport
Airport transfers are reimbursable in economy class.
Ride-hailing receipts must show the trip date, time and amount.

## Exclusions
Alcohol, personal entertainment, minibar charges and traffic fines are not reimbursable.
Upgrades to business class require VP approval and a documented business reason.
