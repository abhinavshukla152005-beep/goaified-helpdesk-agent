# IT Acceptable Use Policy

## Accounts and passwords
Every employee gets a single corporate identity used for email, Jira, Confluence and the HR portal.
Passwords must be at least 14 characters and are rotated every 180 days.
Multi-factor authentication is mandatory on all corporate accounts.
Passwords must never be shared, including with IT staff.

## Software
Only software from the approved catalogue may be installed on corporate laptops.
Requests for new software go through the IT helpdesk with a business justification.
Unapproved browser extensions that request access to page content are prohibited.

## Devices
Corporate laptops are encrypted at rest and must run the company endpoint agent.
Personal devices may access email only through the managed mobile profile.
USB mass storage is disabled by default; exceptions require manager and IT approval.

## Data handling
Company documents are classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted.
Confidential and Restricted data must never be pasted into public AI tools or personal cloud storage.
Customer data may only be processed inside systems approved by the security team.

## Incidents
Any suspected phishing, malware or credential compromise must be reported to security@company.example within 24 hours.
Do not attempt to investigate or remediate a suspected compromise yourself.
Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT immediately so the device can be wiped remotely.

## Helpdesk
IT support is available 09:00-18:00 IST, Monday to Friday.
Priority-1 incidents (security or total outage) are acknowledged within 30 minutes.
Standard requests are acknowledged within 1 working day.
