# Leave & Attendance Policy

## Annual leave
Every full-time employee accrues 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Accrual is 1.5 days per completed month of service.
Employees on probation accrue 1 day per completed month until confirmation.

## Sick leave
Employees get 10 paid sick days per calendar year.
Absences longer than 2 consecutive days require a medical certificate uploaded to the HR portal.

## Casual leave
Employees get 7 paid casual days per calendar year.
Casual leave must be requested at least 1 working day in advance.

## Notice periods
Leave of 2 days or fewer requires 3 working days of notice.
Leave of more than 2 days requires 7 working days of notice.
Unplanned sick leave must be reported to the reporting manager before 10:00 IST on the first day.

## Carry-forward
A maximum of 5 unused annual leave days can be carried into the next calendar year.
Carried-forward days must be used by 31 March or they lapse.
Sick and casual leave do not carry forward.

## Encashment
Annual leave can be encashed only at exit, up to a maximum of 10 days.

## Public holidays
The holiday calendar is published in January each year on the HR portal.
Working on a public holiday requires prior approval and qualifies for a compensatory off.
